Hi, it's Sayem!

A believer of AI bringing positive changes in the world!

I am a senior year student, studying Industrial Engineering at Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST). Besides, I am currently working as a research assistant (intern) at Vision and Learning Lab, UNIST under the supervision of Prof. Seungryul Baek. My current research involves improving human detection of object detection models using thermal images. Before joining Vision and Learning Lab, I worked on bandit algorithms, android development and pose estimation. For details please check my resume.
Along with my research, I am also a member of the club PAIS, which is a student organization, guided by Prof. Bradley Tatar, holds disscussions on AI and its future impact on our society to promote AI awareness among students.
